    How to play

    Survivors

    Restore 4 of the 5 generators to power the exit gate, then escape. Or stay alive until the timer runs out.

    Press E at a generator to open its panel and drag pipes between matching valves without crossing them. Each panel restores a circuit (2 or 3 per generator). The work hums, and every restored circuit clangs loud enough for it to hear.

    Every step makes noise. C crouch is quiet, Shift sprinting is loud. Crouch in tall grass and it can't make you out. F flashlight. E hides you in lockers, tents and outhouses for as long as you can hold your breath. G throws a stone as a decoy. Two hits and you're taken.

    The Monster

    You are blind. A dim grid of light shines from your eyes, and in silence everything looks the same in it, survivors included. Sound gives the world shape: every noise sends out a ripple, and whatever it passes keeps ringing for a moment. Loud ripples faintly trace any survivor they pass over; a crouched step leaves nothing.

    Stand still and you fade into the background for survivors. LMB or Space attack (strike a half-wired generator to rip a circuit out) · Q/RMB sonar shriek (lights up every unhidden survivor within 42 m, but they hear it) · Shift drop to all fours and charge. The ring around your view points to every sound you hear.

    WASD or arrow keys move. Look with the mouse (click to lock the cursor), or drag on the screen to look with a mouse or touch. V toggles monster vision while spectating.
