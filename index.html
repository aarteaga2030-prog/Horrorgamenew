    How to play

    Survivors

    Repair all 3 generators (hold E) to open the exit gate, then escape. Or stay alive until the timer runs out.

    Every step makes noise. C crouch is nearly silent, Shift sprinting is loud. Repairing hums.

    F flashlight. Two hits and you're taken.

    The Monster

    You are blind. You see a flat sonar screen: the world only appears where sound sweeps across it, and survivors only show up when they make noise. Scroll to zoom.

    Stand still and you fade into the background for survivors. LMB or Space attack · Q/RMB sonar shriek (reveals everything nearby, but survivors hear it) · Shift charge.

    WASD or arrow keys move. Look with the mouse (click to lock the cursor), or drag on the screen to look with a mouse or touch. V toggles monster vision while spectating.
